# Physics

Classic problems from a first physics course, each worked from the picture to the formula, with the numbers checked by the program behind the page.

## Statics
- a-box-hanging-from-two-ropes — the tension in each rope from the weight and the two angles, and why a flatter rope pulls harder

## Solids
- graphenes-dirac-cones — tight-binding graphene: the two bands touch at the zone corners, and the cone's slope there is the Fermi velocity 3ta/2

## Quantum mechanics
- transfer-matrices-and-resonant-tunneling — a product of 2 by 2 matrices gives T and R for any stack of flat barriers; T + R = 1 to 1e-15, and two walls that each pass under 1% pass 100% at a resonance
