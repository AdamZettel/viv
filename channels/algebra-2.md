# Algebra 2

Quadratics, systems of equations, exponentials and logarithms, and sequences and series, taught as programs that run and test their own answers. Read a ladder or a demo, then solve the problem after it.

Every post here is one program. A demo shows a fact and tests it. A ladder derives a formula in steps, called rungs, each with its own tests. A problem is a program with unfinished functions and tests that say what they should return; you solve it by filling in the functions until the tests pass, either inside the post or in the post editor. Solutions can be posted too, so worked answers collect here as people solve the problems.
