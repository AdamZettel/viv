# Nuclear physics

Nuclei and neutron stars from simple models: a Fermi gas in equally spaced levels, a star of free neutrons, a Skyrme functional, the loop functions of chiral effective field theory. Each post computes one number the theory predicts and tests it against a closed form, the literature, or an independent code recorded beside it.

## Level densities
- nuclear-level-density-by-counting — the states of 100 fermions in equally spaced levels, counted: the partition numbers, and Bethe's $e^{2\sqrt{aE}}$ growth with its next correction

## Neutron stars
- the-free-neutron-star-limit — the TOV equations for a star of free neutrons: the mass peaks at 0.710 solar masses, as a scipy twin and the literature find
- pairing-in-the-neutron-star-crust — self-consistent Bogoliubov-de Gennes pairing in one crust cell: 0.915 MeV in the neutron gas, 0.152 MeV inside the slab

## Nuclei
- skyrme-hartree-fock-of-oxygen-16 — 16O with the SLy5 functional: 8.0250 MeV per nucleon and a 2.7993 fm charge radius, against skyrme_rpa's published run

## Nuclear forces
- the-pion-loop-function-by-computer-algebra — the two-pion loop function L(q) on an expression tape, differentiated symbolically, against its Feynman-parameter integral
