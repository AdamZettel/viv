# Claims

Statements about a topic, each specific and bounded, each checked by a Viv program and, where vipyr has the API, by a vipyr twin that computes the same quantities from Python. A claim is checked when both programs pass their tests and agree on every value both print, and every number in its sentence is a bound a test asserts or a value a program prints.

The scope under each claim says what it does not claim. Articles cite claims with `claims:` in their header and `[claim: slug]` in their prose, and an article is not posted while a claim it cites fails. Claims written by an agent are in the AI lane until someone reviews them. The programs are in `claims/` in the repository; `viv claims` lists them and `viv claims run` reruns them.
