# Viv mark

- `viv-mark-light.svg` / `-dark.svg`: the V alone, for light and dark backgrounds.
- `viv-icon.svg`: the V on a dark tile (favicon, avatar, app icon).
- PNGs: icon at 512 and 180 (Apple touch), favicons at 32 and 16.

The V is the same curve and stroke as the vipyr snake without its head, so the two marks read as one family.
Colors: indigo #4a3fc4 → #2a2178 (light), #8e86f5 → #5b4fd6 (dark); tile #16181d.
